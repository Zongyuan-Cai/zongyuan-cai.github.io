Zongyuan Cai

I am at Southeast University, in the School of Computer Science and Engineering. My research interests lie in large language models, intelligent agents, and multimodal learning.

I am particularly interested in how LLM-based agents can reason, use tools, and learn from experience across tasks. My recent work explores agent memory and self-evolution for generating scientific animations.

Publications

ManimAgent: Self-Evolving Multimodal Agents for Visual Education

Wenjia Jiang, Zongyuan Cai, Yuanhang Shao, Chenru Wang, Boyan Han, Zhixue Song, Keyu Chen, Shengwei An, Xu Yang, Zhou Yang

arXiv preprint, 2026

[arXiv][PDF][Project page][Code]

A multimodal agent that generates Manim animations from scientific content and learns across tasks through a dual-channel memory bank of successful examples and validated failure patterns, without updating model weights.
