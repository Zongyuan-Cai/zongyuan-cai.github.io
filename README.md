## About · 关于我

My research interests are multi-agent systems and LLM interpretability. I am interested in how agents coordinate and collaborate, and in understanding the reasoning and behavior of large language models.

我的研究兴趣是多智能体系统与大语言模型可解释性，关注智能体之间的协调与协作，以及如何理解大语言模型的推理过程与行为。

## Selected Research · 代表性研究

### [ManimAgent: Self-Evolving Multimodal Agents for Visual Education](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30296)

Wenjia Jiang, **Zongyuan Cai**, Yuanhang Shao, Chenru Wang, Boyan Han, Zhixue Song, Keyu Chen, Shengwei An, Xu Yang, Zhou Yang

**EMNLP 2026 · Poster** · [arXiv:2606.30296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30296)

An agent that turns scientific content into Manim animations and retains useful examples and failure lessons in a dual-channel memory bank. Experience carries across tasks without updating model weights.

通过双通道记忆库积累成功案例与失败教训，让生成科学动画的智能体能够跨任务利用经验，无需更新模型权重。

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30296) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.30296) · [Project Page](https://manimagent.github.io/) · [Code](https://github.com/jwj1342/Paper2Manim)
